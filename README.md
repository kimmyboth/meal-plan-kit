# Meal plan kit

Tell Claude once how you eat: who you cook for, your targets if you track
them, the foods you won't touch, your kitchen, your store and your budget.
Then each week it plans your meals around this week's specials, writes one
prep session you can cook along to without opening a recipe, and puts the
shopping list on your phone.

## What you need

- A **Claude Pro** plan (or higher). The free plan doesn't include Claude Code.
- The **Claude desktop app** for Mac or Windows, signed in:
  https://claude.ai/download
- A Mac if you want the list sent straight to Apple Reminders. On Windows,
  or for Google Keep, you paste it in instead.

## Getting started

Do these one at a time.

1. Download this kit: on the GitHub page, click the green **Code** button,
   then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it and move the folder somewhere you'll keep it, like your Documents
   folder. Rename it **meal-plan** if you like.
3. Open the Claude desktop app and go to the **Code** tab.
4. Start a new session and choose the **meal-plan** folder as its folder.
   If it asks whether you trust the folder, say yes.
5. Type **/get-started** and press Enter. Claude asks you a few questions and
   sets everything up. Answer in your own words. Typos are fine.

## Each week

Open a session in the same folder and type **/plan-week**. Claude asks what's
on this week, checks your store's specials, and gives you:

- the plan for each day
- one prep session, step by step, with every amount, time and temperature
- a shopping list with prices and a total against your budget, sent to
  Reminders or ready to paste into Keep

## Any time

- **/add-recipe**: paste a link, an Instagram or TikTok caption, a screenshot,
  or just a dish name like "my mum's lasagne".
- **/add-receipt**: paste the text from an online order email, or attach
  photos, screenshots or the PDF of a past receipt. The more you add, the
  better it knows your brands and real prices.
- Just tell Claude when something changes, like "we've gone off mushrooms" or
  "budget's $250 now", and it updates your profile.

## Your information

Everything you tell it stays in this folder on your computer: your profile,
recipes, plans and receipts. Planning uses your own Claude plan. Numbers are
Claude's estimates unless they come from your own tracking or a pack label.
They aren't medical or dietary advice.
