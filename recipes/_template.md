## Per serve (est.)

520 kcal · 38g protein · 45g carbs · 18g fat · 7g fibre

## Ingredients

- 500g beef mince
- 1 brown onion (150g), finely diced
- 2 tsp (6g) ground cumin

## Method

1. Heat 1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
   Add the 150g diced onion and cook for 4 minutes until soft.
2. Add the 500g mince and brown for 6–8 minutes, breaking it up as it cooks.
